# Brief

Fill this in before writing scenes. Every number and claim in the video must be in
"Facts" with its source; the video says nothing that is not here.

## Product
What it is, in one sentence.

## Audience and goal
Who watches, where (a stage screen, a feed on mute, a website), and what they should do after.

## Format
- Length: 
- Shape: landscape 16:9 / portrait 9:16 / square
- Languages: 
- Voice: yes / no; if yes, which voice and tone
- Music: generated / a track supplied by the user / none

## Facts
| Fact as it will be said | Source (deck page, URL, document, the user's own words and date) |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Materials
Logo, screenshots, photos, site URL, brand colours and fonts, and where each came from.

## Must not
Claims, words or images to keep out.
