# Brief: Ottoman Empire in 60 seconds

The kit's third example: a history explainer for Reels, captions and music, no voice.

## Product
A short historical explainer: the Ottoman Empire from its founding to its end, every
major period in one minute.

## Audience and goal
Instagram Reels viewers, mostly watching without sound. They should come away with the
arc of six centuries: rise, peak, long retreat, reforms, war, end. The captions carry
the whole story.

## Format
- Length: about 60 s, cut to the music (125 BPM, a bar is 1.92 s); 31 bars = 59.5 s
- Shape: portrait 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps
- Language: Russian
- Voice: none (the person chose captions and music only, 2026-10-28)
- Music: "Tabuk" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0, credited on the end card

## Materials
Paintings, engravings, photographs and a map from Wikimedia Commons (public domain, CC0,
one CC BY 4.0 photo); authors and links in `assets/sources.json`. Each picture
carries a small on-screen label with what it is, taken from its Commons page: the Osman
portrait is an "imaginary portrait, 18th century"; "Mehmed II entering Constantinople" is
by Fausto Zonaro (1854–1929), not of the period; Hackert's painting shows the Battle of
Chesma (1770); the engraving from The Graphic shows the opening of the first Ottoman
parliament (1877); the photograph is "Turk soldiers, Gallipoli" (Bain News Service).

## Must not
- No Armenian genocide or other 1915 events: left out by agreement with the person,
  2026-10-28 (the video is about the state and its periods).
- No numbers beyond the table; no modern political claims.
