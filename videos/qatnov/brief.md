# Brief: Qatnov

**This is the kit's example. The service, its figures and everything said about it are
invented.** The end card says so. "Qatnov" is an ordinary Uzbek word (a regular service,
as in "aviaqatnov"); a web search on 2026-11-03 found no app or company by that name.

It started from the structure of a real pitch video (app screens redrawn in code and acted
out, figures as huge counters, a narrator) but has its own look, chosen on 2026-11-03 after
the person asked for something less like the source and more dynamic: Samarkand-tile
colours (lapis ground, turquoise, saffron), pointed arches (iwans) as frames for footage and
the phone, an eight-point star lattice as texture, a dashed road line, a light iOS-style app
in a titanium phone, camera moves over the phone, and cuts on the bars of a lazgi track.

## Product
Qatnov, an app for intercity trips in Uzbekistan: book a seat in a shared car or a bus
between cities in advance, see the departure time and price before you go, pay by card,
travel with a QR ticket. Drivers get passengers through the same app instead of waiting
at the station until the car is full.

## Audience and goal
People who look at the kit's examples (developers, founders, makers) and, in the story
of the video, passengers in Uzbekistan who travel between cities. After 75 seconds they
should see a product demo in the style of a startup pitch: a problem, the app acted out,
traction figures, a map, a closing card.

## Format
- Length: about 75 s
- Shape: landscape 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps
- Language: Uzbek voice (Latin script), Russian subtitles at the bottom of the frame;
  on-screen interface and captions in Uzbek
- Voice: Google Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, voice Sulafat, through OpenRouter; chosen by ear
  from five samples by the person (2026-11-03). The Uzbek lines and on-screen text were
  written by the agent and checked by a native Uzbek speaker (the person, 2026-11-03).
- Music: an instrumental in the style of Khorezm lazgi (surnay, dutar, doira), 120 BPM,
  generated with Google Lyria 3 Pro through OpenRouter; the person chose it by ear from three
  generated samples (2026-11-03) after rejecting incompetech tracks. Scenes are whole bars
  (2 s) and cut without crossfades.
- Footage: Pexels clips of Tashkent (Hazrati Imam from a drone), Samarkand (Registan),
  Bukhara (Po-i-Kalyan through a café window) and a desert road from above; no recognisable
  people in focus.

## Materials
- No logo exists: a word mark set in the display face, with a turquoise arch and a road
  running through it, drawn in SVG.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Inter (the kit's default) for the app and the
  subtitles; both OFL. Bricolage has no `ʻ` (U+02BB), so on-screen text writes oʻ and gʻ
  as `o‘ g‘` (U+2018); the voice lines keep `ʻ`, which the voice reads correctly.
- Colours: lapis `#0D1B3E`, turquoise `#1FB5A6` (`#0E9C8F` in the app), saffron `#F5B53F`,
  cream `#F6EFE2`.
- Map: region outlines of Uzbekistan from an openly licensed source, credited on screen.

## Must not
- No Beeline, Beepul or VEON names, colours, logos or screens.
- No real taxi, bus or travel company (Yandex Go, MyTaxi, Uzbekistan Airways, Afrosiyob
  trains) named or shown.
- No price comparisons and no claims about safety statistics.
- No stock shots of recognisable people.
