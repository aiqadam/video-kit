# Brief: Chilim Usta

**This is the kit's example. The brand, the workshop and every fact below are invented.**
They stand in for what a real user would supply; the end card says the brand is fictional.

## Product
Chilim Usta, a workshop in Tashkent that makes hookahs to order by hand: blown glass,
ceramics, painted patterns. You choose the colour, pattern and engraving on your phone.

## Audience and goal
People in Uzbekistan who want a hookah as a crafted object or a gift. Seen in a social feed
and on the workshop's site. They should leave thinking "made by a master, made for me".

## Format
- Length: about 37 seconds
- Shape: landscape 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps
- Language: Russian, with an Uzbek phrase and an English closing line
- Voice: ElevenLabs eleven_v3 (it has Uzbek; multilingual_v2 does not), voice George
- Music: generated, "hijaz" style (Central Asian and Middle Eastern colour)

## Materials

## Must not
- No smoke, tobacco, flavours or people smoking: the video sells the craft object. Tobacco
  advertising is restricted in Uzbekistan and on most ad platforms.
- No photos of real, recognisable craftspeople: they would read as working for this brand.
- No prices: there are none in the brief.
