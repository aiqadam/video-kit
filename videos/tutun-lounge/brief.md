# Brief: TUTUN lounge

**This is the kit's example. The lounge and everything said about it are invented.**
The end card says so.

## Product
TUTUN lounge, a hookah lounge in Tashkent ("tutun" is Uzbek for smoke). The draw is the
ritual: tobacco mixed to your taste, a bowl packed by hand, coals heated to an even glow.

## Audience and goal
Adults in Tashkent choosing where to spend the evening; seen in Instagram and Telegram.
After 40 seconds they should want to go tonight.

## Format
- Length: 42 seconds, 15 bars of the music (86 BPM, a bar is 2.79 s); every cut on a downbeat
- Shape: landscape 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps
- Language: Russian, with an Uzbek welcome
- Voice: short lines, low and unhurried; ElevenLabs eleven_v3
- Music: "Desert City" by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0), percussion and dulcimer groove

## Must not
- No minors anywhere in the footage (a Pexels clip titled "teen smokes shisha" was left out).
- 18+ mark and a health warning on the end card: tobacco advertising rules in Uzbekistan
  and most platforms require them.
- No prices, no promotions: none in the brief.
