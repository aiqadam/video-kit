# The brief

`videos/<name>/brief.md` is the video's source of truth: what it says and where each
statement comes from. The template has the sections; fill them before the scenes.

## What to find out

Take what the request and the materials already answer; ask only for the rest, in one
message, as a short numbered list the person can answer in a line each.

1. **Product.** What it is, in one sentence. Its site, deck, app or repository.
2. **Audience and place.** Who watches, and where: a stage screen, a social feed that
   autoplays without sound (then captions carry the story), a website, an investor email.
3. **Goal.** What the viewer should think or do afterwards.
4. **Shape.** Length; landscape, portrait or square; resolution if there is a spec
   (a venue screen, an ad platform).
5. **Language(s).** One, several versions, or one language with inserts in another.
6. **Voice.** Narrated or not; voice, gender and tone; provider if they have one.
7. **Music.** Generated, their own track, or none.
8. **Facts.** The numbers and claims the video should make, each with its source.
9. **Materials.** Logo, brand colours and fonts, screenshots, photos, footage.
10. **Must not.** Claims, words, images or competitors to keep out; legal caveats that
    must stay on screen.

Do not invent answers to 8–10. Defaults are fine for the rest (landscape, 30–60 s,
the language of the conversation, generated music) if you say which ones you chose.

## Recording facts

One row per fact, worded as the video will say it, with its source: a deck page, a URL
with the date you read it, a document, or "the person, in this conversation, <date>".
When the person corrects a figure, replace the row and note the date. When a figure comes
from outside the person's materials, the video shows its source on screen.

## Sensitive subjects

Regulated products (tobacco, alcohol, finance, health, gambling) have advertising rules:
note the ones that apply under "Must not" and keep required caveats on screen. An example
or fictional brand says so on its end card.
